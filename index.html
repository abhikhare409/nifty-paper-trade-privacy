Privacy Policy - Nifty Paper Trade

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nifty Paper Trade ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android application.

1. About the App

Nifty Paper Trade is a virtual trading and educational practice application. It allows users to view market prices and charts and practice with simulated orders, virtual positions, and hypothetical profit and loss. The app does not execute real stock market trades and is not a brokerage service.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process the following information:

  • Account information: Information made available through Google Sign-In, such as your name, email address, and profile image.
  • Mobile number: Your phone number if you choose to provide it during profile setup.
  • App activity: Virtual orders, simulated positions, watchlist selections, and related practice data.
  • Technical information: Device, diagnostic, and usage information that may be collected by services used to operate the app, provide advertisements, or maintain security.

The information actually collected depends on the app's configuration and the third-party services enabled.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Sign users in and maintain their profiles.
  • Provide virtual trading features, charts, simulated positions, and practice history.
  • Display market information and app features.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve app performance.
  • Display advertisements where advertising services are enabled.

4. Market Data and Virtual Trading

Market prices and other financial information displayed in the app may be provided by third-party data sources. Availability, accuracy, completeness, and timeliness may vary. Market data may be delayed and may be subject to provider terms and permissions.

Orders, balances, positions, and profit or loss shown in the app are simulated for practice. They do not represent actual investments or real brokerage orders. The app does not guarantee profits or investment returns.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Sign-In, advertising services, and market-data providers. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review their policies for details about their data practices.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Advertising

The app may display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements. If an advertising provider is enabled, it may collect or use device identifiers and other information as described in its own privacy policy.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers to operate app features, or disclosed when required by applicable law, to protect security, or to address misuse. The information shared depends on the services and features enabled in the app.

8. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements and the capabilities of the services used. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact the developer so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may choose not to provide optional profile information. You may contact the developer to ask about accessing, correcting, or deleting personal information, subject to applicable law and necessary retention requirements. Some information may need to be managed through the relevant third-party service.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
