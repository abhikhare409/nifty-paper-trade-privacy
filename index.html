Privacy Policy - Nifty Paper Trade

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nifty Paper Trade ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android application.

1. About the App

Nifty Paper Trade is a virtual trading and educational practice application. It allows users to view market prices and charts and practice with simulated orders, virtual positions, and hypothetical profit and loss. The app does not execute real stock market trades and is not a brokerage service.

2. Information We Collect

The app processes the following information:

  • Account information (Google Sign-In): your name, email address, and profile photo link, as made available by your Google account. Signing in with Google is required to use the app.
  • Mobile number: you are asked to enter a mobile number once, after signing in, and it is required to use the app. The number is not verified (no OTP is sent).
  • Profile records: your Google account ID, the date you joined, the date of your last login, and the app version you use.
  • Practice data: virtual orders, simulated positions, balances, watchlist selections, and trade history. This data is stored on your device.
  • Technical information: device, diagnostic, and usage information, including an advertising identifier and IP address, that may be collected by the services used to operate the app, show advertisements, or keep it secure.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Sign you in and maintain your profile.
  • Store the mobile number you provide with your profile, and contact you about your account or about support requests.
  • Provide virtual trading features, charts, simulated positions, and practice history.
  • Display market information and app features.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, require app updates when necessary, and improve app performance.
  • Display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

4. Market Data and Virtual Trading

Market prices and other financial information displayed in the app may be provided by third-party data sources. Availability, accuracy, completeness, and timeliness may vary. Market data may be delayed and may be subject to provider terms and permissions.

Orders, balances, positions, and profit or loss shown in the app are simulated for practice. They do not represent actual investments or real brokerage orders. The app does not provide investment advice and does not guarantee profits or investment returns.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Remote Config): for login, storing your profile record, and delivering app update settings. Google Privacy Policy | Firebase Privacy Information
  • Unity Ads: for showing rewarded advertisements. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Market-data servers: the app connects to servers that provide option prices. These servers may receive technical information such as your IP address when the app requests data.

6. Advertising

The app displays rewarded advertisements through Unity Ads. Unity may collect or use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and other information to show and measure advertisements, as described in its privacy policy.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information is processed by the service providers listed above to operate app features, and may be disclosed when required by applicable law, to protect security, or to address misuse.

8. Data Retention and Security

Your profile record (name, email, profile photo link, mobile number, and the related records listed in section 2) is kept in Google Firebase until you delete your account. Practice data stored on your device stays there until you reset the paper account, delete your account, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access to profile records is restricted by security rules, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact the developer so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Your Choices, Account Deletion and Data Requests

Delete your account in the app: open the Profile tab and tap "Delete account". This deletes your login and your profile record (including your mobile number) from our systems, and clears the paper-trading data on your device. You may be asked to confirm your Google account once more.

Delete your data by request: if you cannot use the app, email us at the address in section 12 from the email address linked to your account, and ask for your account and data to be deleted. You may also contact us to ask about accessing or correcting your personal information. Some information may need to be managed through the relevant third-party service, such as your Google account.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
